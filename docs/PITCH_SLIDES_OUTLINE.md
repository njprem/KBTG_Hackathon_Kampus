# Three-minute pitch outline

Working outline for the team. Keep product explanation here; the clickable banking prototype should show only the transaction, its current status, the reason for a pause, and actions the user can take. All people, accounts, amounts, and outcomes shown in the demo are fictional.

| Slide | Time | What the audience sees | Speaker point |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| 1. Problem and user | 30 sec | A first jobber about to transfer money under pressure | A rushed transfer is hard to reconsider once sent. The user needs a clear pause at the moment of decision. Do not claim a measured fraud rate without a source. |
| 2. How it works | 55 sec | Simple sequence: transfer review, warning, trusted person's view, sender decision | The user sets a monthly threshold and chooses a protection level. A relevant transfer is paused before any money is sent. Explain that the trusted person sees a masked amount, not the exact value. |
| 3. Live prototype | 70 sec | Screen recording or controlled walkthrough of the ordinary banking flow | Show the red danger state, the reason for the pause, the two-minute Warn timer, the no-response recommendation, and the second confirmation. For a live three-minute demo, use the clearly labeled demo time-skip. Briefly distinguish the stronger approval-required level and its separate pending recovery route. |
| 4. Value and next test | 25 sec | One plain takeaway and a small test plan | The intended value is more time and a second perspective before a concerning transfer. This is a hypothesis, not a measured outcome. Test whether two minutes helps and whether a contact can make a sound decision with the exact amount hidden. |
